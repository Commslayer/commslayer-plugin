# Worked guidance examples

These are example merchant policies, not defaults for every store. Use them to see the shape of good guidance: a short intent trigger, and instructions written as scenarios and conditional steps. Take timeframes, deadlines and resolutions from the merchant's own policy.

## WISMO: regional delivery timeframes

**Title:** WISMO: Regional delivery timeframes

**Trigger:** Customer asks about order location, delivery status, shipping updates or tracking information. Includes questions about delivery timing, order progress and shipping delays.

**Instructions**

### Scenario 1: No tracking available yet

Trigger: Customer asks for tracking or order status and no tracking number or URL is available.

If the order was created two or fewer business days ago:

* Explain that tracking will be available once the order ships, usually within 24 hours of ordering.
* Ask the customer to check back later for updates.

If the order was created more than two business days ago:

* Apologize for the shipping delay.
* Escalate to an agent.

### Scenario 2: Tracking available, not delivered

Trigger: Customer asks for tracking or order status. The order has tracking information but is not marked delivered.

Delivery timeframes by region:

* USA: 5 to 9 business days.
* Canada: 5 to 9 business days.
* UK: 5 to 7 business days.
* Australia: 5 to 9 business days.
* Other countries: 10 to 15 business days.

Share the estimated delivery date if available. Otherwise, use the order date plus the regional delivery window to assess whether the order is on time. Use the end of the window to assess delays.

If the order is on time:

* Confirm the status and share tracking information.

If the order is delayed by two business days or less:

* Ask the customer to allow an additional two business days for delivery.

If the order is delayed by more than two business days:

* Escalate to an agent.

### Scenario 3: Marked delivered, not received

Trigger: Customer reports that the order is marked delivered but they haven't received it.

Ask the customer to check:

* Whether a neighbor, roommate or doorman accepted the package.
* The shipping address.
* Whether the carrier has additional delivery details.
* Whether the package was left in a discreet location.

Next steps:

* Ask the customer to wait two business days after the posted delivery date.
* Share available tracking information and the shipping address.
* If the customer has completed these checks and it has been at least two business days since the order was marked delivered, escalate to an agent.

## Damaged or defective item

Use the merchant's existing damage policy. Don't copy deadlines or resolution promises from another store.

**Title:** Damaged or defective item

**Trigger:** Customer reports that an item arrived damaged, broken or defective.

**Instructions**

* If it isn't already clear, ask which item was damaged.
* Request photos of the damaged item if they haven't already been provided.
* Search knowledge for the damage policy and explain the available options.
* Process the resolution according to the policy and available actions. Escalate when a human needs to handle it.
